Our Story

Vanilla Sky is a 100% Tunisian perfume brand born from a passion for creating unique fragrances that tell a story. We combine traditional Tunisian craftsmanship with modern perfumery techniques to create exceptional scents.

Our collection includes luxury dupes that capture the essence of high-end fragrances at accessible prices, as well as exclusive creations signed "Vanilla Sky" that are unique to our brand.

Each fragrance is crafted with care, using high-quality ingredients to ensure longevity and a beautiful scent journey from first spray to dry down.
